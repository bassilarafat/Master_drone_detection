\# Drone Detection Master Thesis Research Plan



\## Title

A New Computer Vision and Deep Learning Approach for Real-Time Drone Detection



\## Main Objective

Develop an accurate and real-time deep learning based drone detection system.



\## Research Problem

Detecting small drones in complex visual environments remains challenging due to:

\- Small object size

\- Limited visual information

\- Background complexity

\- Real-time constraints



\## Current Phase

Environment preparation completed.



\## Next Steps

1\. Literature review

2\. Dataset selection

3\. Baseline detector implementation

4\. Error analysis

5\. Proposed method development

6\. Ablation study

7\. Final evaluation
